## Design system

- Canvas 1080 × 1440 portrait. Side margins 96 px.
- Serif: Bodoni Moda (high contrast, optical size pinned at 30–40 so hairlines survive on a phone). One italic word or phrase per headline.
- Sans: Archivo, bold and tracked uppercase for places, labels and meta, regular for supporting copy (32–44 px).
- Colors: paper #F4EFE6, ink #1B1815, muted #5F574E, ivory on photos #F5EEE2, burgundy #7A2E34 (carousel 5 only).
- Interior slides: meta row over a 2 px rule, big serif headline, ruled supporting blocks, a thin 520 px swipe arrow bottom-right, and the handle bottom-left in 22 px tracked caps.
- Covers: full-bleed photo with a soft dark overlay (about 35% to 70% top to bottom) and oversized ivory serif type.
- Closing slides: centered typography, short CTA, name, brokerage and handle at the bottom.

### Slide 2: decision

- **Meta row:** BEFORE YOU LIST  |  DECISION 01 OF 05
- **Headline:** Visible *maintenance*
- **Number:** 01
- **Label:** FIX FIRST
- **Buyers notice:** Drips, stains, sticky doors, cracked caulk, a dead outlet. Small things make buyers wonder what they can't see.
- **The fix:** Walk the house with a notepad and clear the punch list: leaks, caulk, burned-out bulbs, loose handles. Service the furnace.
- **Leave it when:** Older systems that still work. Disclose their age and price for it instead of replacing them.
- **Footer:** @johnspilotros + swipe arrow
- **Layout:** Off-white. Italic serif number top-right, serif headline, then a "Fix first / If needed" badge. Three ruled rows: Buyers notice · The fix · Leave it when.

### Slide 3: decision

- **Meta row:** BEFORE YOU LIST  |  DECISION 02 OF 05
- **Headline:** How rooms *read* in photos
- **Number:** 02
- **Label:** FIX FIRST
- **Buyers notice:** Most buyers meet your home online first. Each room needs to say what it's for at a glance.
- **The fix:** Clear the counters, take out about a third of the furniture, give each room one job, make beds with light bedding, and keep pets and family photos out of frame.
- **Leave it when:** Full staging in a home that's already well furnished. Edit what you have first.
- **Footer:** @johnspilotros + swipe arrow
- **Layout:** Same decision layout.

### Slide 4: decision

- **Meta row:** BEFORE YOU LIST  |  DECISION 03 OF 05
- **Headline:** Lighting
- **Number:** 03
- **Label:** FIX FIRST
- **Buyers notice:** Dim rooms feel smaller in person and in photos, and mismatched bulbs make rooms look patchy.
- **The fix:** Put the same warm-white bulb (2700–3000K) everywhere, replace every dead bulb, open the blinds, and swap the one fixture that dates the room.
- **Leave it when:** Plain fixtures that work. Clean them and move on.
- **Footer:** @johnspilotros + swipe arrow
- **Layout:** Same decision layout.

### Slide 5: decision

- **Meta row:** BEFORE YOU LIST  |  DECISION 04 OF 05
- **Headline:** Paint *touch-ups*
- **Number:** 04
- **Label:** IF NEEDED
- **Buyers notice:** Scuffed trim, patched holes that show, one very loud accent wall.
- **The fix:** Patch and touch up trim and doors. Repaint only the rooms that are scuffed or bold, in a warm neutral.
- **Leave it when:** Walls that are already neutral and clean. A whole-house repaint isn't required.
- **Footer:** @johnspilotros + swipe arrow
- **Layout:** Same decision layout with the outlined "If needed" badge.

### Slide 6: decision
